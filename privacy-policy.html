Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Who We Are

Xebra Holdings LLC ("Xebra," "we," "us," or "our") is a Michigan limited liability company based in the Kalamazoo/Battle Creek area. We provide SMS-based automation services — including missed-call-to-text, appointment booking, lead follow-up, and review request messaging — to home service businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information generated when you communicate with us or with businesses using our services:

  • Contact information — such as your name, phone number, and email address, provided when you call, text, email, fill out a form, or book an appointment.
  • Communication records — the content and metadata of SMS messages, calls, and emails exchanged through our systems, such as timestamps and delivery status.
  • Service and appointment details — information related to service requests, quotes, and scheduled appointments.
  • Technical information — basic website usage data such as browser type and pages visited, if you use our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to missed calls and inquiries with follow-up text messages;
  • Schedule, confirm, and remind you about appointments;
  • Follow up on service requests and quotes;
  • Send review requests after completed jobs;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Maintain and improve our services;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

SMS/Text Messaging

Our services involve sending transactional text messages — such as replies to missed calls, appointment confirmations and reminders, follow-ups on service inquiries, and review requests. You will only receive text messages if you have initiated contact (for example, by calling or texting a business we serve) or otherwise provided consent to receive them.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or reply HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions for full SMS program disclosures.

How We Share Information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Beyond the above, we may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — with vendors and platforms that support our operations (such as our messaging and scheduling platform), solely so they can perform services on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, regulation, legal process, or a governmental request.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of text messages — reply STOP to any message at any time.
  • Access, correction, or deletion — contact us using the information below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
